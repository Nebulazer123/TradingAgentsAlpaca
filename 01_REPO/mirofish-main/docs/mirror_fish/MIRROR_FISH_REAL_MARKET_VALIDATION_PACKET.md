## TradingAgents Ingestion Order

1. Ingest broker/API evidence first: Alpaca, Robinhood, Schwab, Fidelity, Webull, IBKR, and tastytrade if available. Normalize rejection codes, buying-power/margin-deficit semantics, PDT/day-trade-count fields, support/status events, and confirmed fills before any market signal is eligible.
2. Ingest macro calendar and cross-asset market data second: employment/jobs, CPI, PPI, Treasury auctions, yields/rates, Fed/FOMC repricing, oil/geopolitical shocks, VIX/term structure, and SPY/QQQ intraday bars. Validate macro attribution before labeling any move as retail/PDT-driven.
3. Ingest options and microstructure third: SPY/QQQ 0DTE volume, IV/OI, dealer gamma/pinning, bid/ask spreads, order-book depth, cancellation/synchronization proxies, and ETF/futures confirmation.
4. Ingest social, AI/bot/developer, company/executive, and institutional narrative layers last. Use them to explain or warn, not to originate execution.
5. Before acting, TradingAgents must validate all three hard gates: broker execution is real rather than blocked, macro attribution is not dominant, and options/institutional liquidity confirms rather than fades the signal. If any hard gate fails or remains unknown, the Step 4 report stays advisory context only and should not generate trade instructions.
