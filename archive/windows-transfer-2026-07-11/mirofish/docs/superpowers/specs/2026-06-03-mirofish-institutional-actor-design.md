# MiroFish Institutional Actor Design

Date: 2026-06-03

## Goal

The MiroFish PDT/intraday-margin run must simulate a market society, not only a retail forum. The run should include actors that trade, actors that shape rules, actors that shape narratives, actors that operate trading infrastructure, and actors that absorb or fade retail flow.

## Design

Use a multi-layer actor ecology in the reality seed and simulation prompt:

- Retail and individual traders: new day traders, PDT-confused accounts, AI-bot users, prompt-bot users, options/0DTE traders, cash-account settlement-confused users, FOMO buyers, panic sellers, and risk-managed experienced individuals.
- Broker/platform/clearing actors: Robinhood, Alpaca/API users, Fidelity, Schwab, Interactive Brokers, Webull, tastytrade/tastylive, margin desks, clearing/risk teams, support teams, status pages, API teams, and compliance/legal reviewers.
- Government/regulator/policy actors: FINRA, SEC market-structure staff, Federal Reserve/FOMC macro voices, Treasury auction context, congressional/policy-maker commentary, and investor-education offices.
- Institutional investor and market-structure actors: market makers, liquidity providers, ETF/index desks, prop desks, quant funds, hedge funds, asset managers, volatility desks, sell-side/electronic-execution desks, and risk managers.
- Media/narrative actors: financial media outlets, YouTube/TikTok explainers, newsletters, finfluencers, Reddit/X communities, headline aggregators, broker education pages, and data-dashboard accounts.
- Developer/automation/tech actors: broker API developers, bot-framework maintainers, TradingView/script users, AI-agent builders, prompt-bot operators, autonomous-broker integrators, open-source trading communities, infrastructure vendors, tech executives, fintech founders, Apple WWDC participants, semiconductor/AI-infrastructure executives, and investor-relations teams.

## Cross-Layer Dynamics

The simulation should preserve feedback loops across layers:

- policy language becomes broker UI text
- broker UI text becomes retail screenshots
- screenshots become influencer and media narratives
- narratives become novice order flow
- novice order flow becomes market-maker, quant, and ETF-desk signal
- liquidity response becomes retail conspiracy or confirmation narrative
- regulator/broker clarification either cools or intensifies the next wave

## Acceptance Criteria

- The final upload prompt explicitly names all required actor layers.
- The stage 01 ontology or stage 02 generated profiles include first-class non-retail actors.
- The final report requires separate sections for policy/regulator, institutional liquidity, media narrative, developer/API behavior, company/tech executive narrative, and retail archetypes.
- The real run remains advisory only and feeds TradingAgents as validation tasks and risk gates, not direct trade instructions.
