# Intraday-Margin Effective-Date Correction - 2026-08-11

## Superseded claim

Earlier runtime text and historical planning material treated June 4, 2026 as a
universal effective date for FINRA's replacement of the pattern day trader
framework. It also cited FINRA Notice 25-14, which concerns a different subject.
Those claims are superseded for current operation.

## Current evidence-based treatment

SEC Release 34-105226 approved SR-FINRA-2025-017 on April 14, 2026. The order
states that FINRA will announce the effective date in a separate Regulatory
Notice and permits a phased implementation period. No account is treated as
migrated merely because the calendar has passed June 4.

The runtime status is
`approved_pending_finra_notice_or_broker_adoption`. Account-specific activation
defaults to `unknown`; current restrictions remain in force until fresh broker
evidence or an explicit operator-attested adoption record proves otherwise.

Historical plans and reports remain unchanged for provenance. This notice and
the August 11 catch-up report identify the corrected current interpretation.
