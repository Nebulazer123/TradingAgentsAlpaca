# TradingAgents through the day, night and weekend

## The state now

All ten TradingAgents automations are paused. Their saved times describe the
intended cadence; they do not establish scheduled execution. Live control is
frozen, paper submission defaults to off, concurrency remains 1, and overall
readiness is unestablished. The September source collections are retained
observations with their actual capture times. They are not a current broker,
price, learning, or economic qualification refresh.

The following explains the designed operation. A future owner-approved observer
phase could make seven analysis roles eligible for activation after the evidence
and deployment gates. The market supervisor, wake controller and sleep
controller would remain paused. Manual qualification has its own ordered chain.
Local services and explicitly authorized manual work have their own state;
pausing these ten records is not a statement that the whole computer is idle.

## Night and early morning

At 03:30 Central on weekdays, designed overnight research reads allowed market,
company, news and prior decision evidence. Analysts produce role packets;
researchers compare bull and bear theses; the trader proposes a decision and
risk roles challenge it. Deterministic policy evaluates the result. The output
is an evidence-backed research plan, ranked candidates and unresolved gaps.
A proposed trade remains a proposal. The graph, a restored checkpoint, and a
research packet cannot issue approval or bypass an execution gate.

The one-ticker manual qualifier is deliberately capped. A genuine registered
benchmark separately measures whether models improve source accuracy or resolved
forecast quality enough to justify their cost. The current collection's 43
symbols do not establish the eligible ranked top-100/75/50 study universe.
Forecasts require legitimate decision/event mappings and source-bound future
outcomes before they can enter learning or economic conclusions.

The saved wake-controller time is 06:45. That record remains paused even in the
future seven-role observer phase. Its designed job concerns schedule health and
status, not stock selection or execution authority. No overnight or controller
job is scheduled by this readiness work.

## Before the market opens

At 07:03, the designed self-healer checks reliability and produces a bounded
handoff and repair plan. Its later weekday checks are 09:03, 11:03, 13:03 and
15:03. A diagnosis is evidence for a repair decision. The qualification chain
uses a no-execute handoff and plan; it does not allow repairs to silently change
a frozen candidate or an already-started clean session.

At 08:10, preopen validation checks the overnight evidence, relevant fresh
account/market inputs when separately authorized, calendar, restrictions,
reconciliation and control posture. It emits a validation packet with precise
blockers. Missing or stale prerequisites keep the dependent operation closed.
The wrapper always treats preopen as dry-run, including when it inherits an
action environment flag, and does not drain queued notifications.

At 08:20, and hourly through 14:20, the designed safety sentinel checks integrity,
freshness and the chain's bindings. It escalates uncertain diagnoses. It does
not trade or promote a strategy. Its output feeds BOARD and operational review.

## During the regular session

The saved market-supervisor cadence is 08:35 through 14:35, hourly. This
order-capable automation remains paused in both the current state and the future
observer phase. Manual qualification uses an explicit hourly dry-run. That
packet can report candidates, risk gates, proposed actions and unresolved issues
without placing an order or delivering queued mail.

The paper tournament's designed time is 09:10. A research plan feeds a distinct
paper root with immutable inputs, evaluation protocol, clock/calendar checks,
lease and reconciliation. The default is dry-run. Optional paper submission
requires a separate owner record and every submission gate; a repair day cannot
submit. Paper outcomes cannot establish live authority.

At 08:50, and hourly through 14:50, BOARD reviews the bound sentinel, supervisor
and portfolio/loss evidence. For TSM, the review must state the allowed exit
reason, supporting source, thesis status and confidence. HOLD and unresolved
are valid conclusions. A BOARD recommendation remains non-authorizing. An old
July GO or historical live-enabled promotion row cannot establish current
readiness; supersession must use the accepted writer after its prerequisites.

Forecast resolution is an evidence operation. Legitimate mappings identify the
original forecast and decision event; accepted prices and corporate actions
establish the outcome. The existing ledger is backed up and reconciled to a
fixed point. Missing, stale or ambiguous rows remain pending with reasons;
stored outcomes are not rewritten. Reports distinguish raw forecast rows from
unique events, market dates and quality-qualified observations.

## After the close

At 15:30, the designed daily report combines the day's exact packets, proposals,
submissions, reconciliation, loss review, BOARD disposition, learning availability
and gaps. During qualification it stays local. Sending it or draining an outbox
requires separate delivery authority.

The saved sleep-controller time is 16:45 on weekdays. That record remains paused
in the future observer phase too. Its design is a status/cadence function, not a
permission to rearm live control. Market hours, holidays and shortened sessions
come from authenticated session evidence at execution, not these clock labels.

## Weekends and holidays

The ten saved schedules use weekdays; none defines weekend runs. A weekday
holiday still requires the market-calendar/session checks and cannot qualify as
a clean regular market session. The self-healer's saved cadence is a weekday
market-session design; it does not promise continuous weekend coverage.

Authorized offline research, source fixes, receipt inspection and documentation
can continue while inputs are unavailable. A source repair does not produce
elapsed market outcomes. Development, validation and the separately released
holdout remain ordered, source-bound economic phases. A reproducible negative
result can qualify the system's evidence path; missing outcomes cannot.

## The manual six-session campaign

The saved automation times are not the command order for acceptance. Use the
existing future-only runbook in
`.superpowers/sdd/2026-08-17-clean-day-qualification-and-five-day-paper-trial/task-4-report.md`.
Start the day before any other stage and immediately prove all ten paused
records. Bind every stage to the same authenticated session and start object.

| Ordered work | Evidence that moves to the next stage |
| --- | --- |
| Start and paused-record proof | New start object; immediately bound paused-state evidence |
| Capped overnight graph and premarket brief | Exact plan and brief packets |
| Preopen validation and hourly dry-run | Fresh validation and non-submitting supervisor packet |
| Bound safety sentinel and broker reconciliation | Same-day integrity and reconciliation receipts |
| Loss review and BOARD | Supported exit/thesis/confidence assessment and non-authorizing decision |
| No-execute self-heal handoff and plan | Exact diagnostic and proposal packets |
| Local-only daily report | Bound report without delivery |
| Distinct paper-root initialization and dry-run | Bound paper packet; submission defaults off |
| One complete manifest and one adjudication | Exactly the twelve required stage entries and a clean day decision |

The twelve entries are overnight research, premarket brief, preopen validation,
hourly supervisor, safety sentinel, broker reconciliation, loss review, execution
BOARD, self-heal handoff, self-heal plan, daily report and paper tournament.

Complete one clean qualifier first. Then initialize a separate five-day root and
complete five additional distinct clean regular market sessions. The qualifier
is not Day 1. A failed or incomplete day resets the streak and requires repair
plus a new qualifier. Preserve all failure receipts. After clean Day 5, finalize
the lease, prove further submissions reject, verify paused records and emit one
terminal readiness candidate or no-go. This proves operational continuity;
profitability and live readiness require their own evidence and authority.
